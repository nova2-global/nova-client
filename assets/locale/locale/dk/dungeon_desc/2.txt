#Task 1:
Destroy the metin stones until the 
desired metins stones have spawned.

$Note:
Every metin stone has a special effect ,
which will be increased by the number of 
the same metins stone.

The amount of HP regeneration of the
dragon will be increased by the metin of
arrogance.

Dragon Damage will be increased by the
Metin of vengeance.

HP regeneration speed will be increased
by the Metin of solitude.

Dragon Defence will be increased by the 
Metin of mountain.
