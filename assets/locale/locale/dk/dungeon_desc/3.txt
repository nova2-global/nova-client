#Stage 1:
After activating the run on the Ice Lion ,
Kill all the monsters to proceed to the 
next level.

#Stage 2:
Kill monsters until you drop the real 
Frost Key to proceed to the next level.

#Stage 3:
Kill all monsters.

#Stage 4:
Kill the 2 waves of monsters to proceed.

#Stage 5:
Kill monsters that will drop north stars,
use them to open the seals on the arctic 
Cubes,Seals have to be opened in the 
correct order.

#Stage 6:
Kill all the monsters and destroy the 
Metin of Cold.

#Stage 7:
Find and defeat Szel, The lord of Ice - 
Find and kill the coorect Szel.

#Stage 8:
Kill monsters until you drop the correct 
Frostflower key to proceed to next level.

#Stage 9:
Destroy the North Dragon Pillar to enter 
the boss room talk to the Ice Lion.

#Boss Room:
Defeat Nemere, King of Frost and Ice!
