#Ground floor:
Destroy all the Metin.
Once the Metins are destroyed, you will be teleported to the 1st floor.

#Floor 1:
Kill Death Reaper to obtain Zin-Bong-In Key.
Use the key on Sa-Soe To Spawn the next Boss.
Kill Elite Vile Demon King to proceed to the final boss.

#Floor 2:
Kill Blue Death, The Ruler of the Demon Tower.
