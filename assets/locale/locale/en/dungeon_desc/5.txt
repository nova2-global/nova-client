#Stage 1:
Kill monsters that will drop north stars, use them to open the seals on the arctic Cubes,
Seals have to be opened in the correct order.

#Stage 2:
Kill all the monsters and destroy the Metin of Cold.

#Stage 3:
Find and defeat Szel, The lord of Ice.

#Stage 4:
Kill monsters until you drop the correct frostflower key to proceed to next level.

#Stage 5:
Destroy the North Dragon Pillar. To enter the boss room talk to the Ice Lion.

#Boss Room:
Defeat Nemere, King of Frost and Ice!!!
