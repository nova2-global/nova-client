### Dungeon ticket
No ticket is needed to enter this dungeon.

### Bonus needed
Strong against undead.

--- {style=ornate}

### Phase 1
Destroy all the Metins of Ember and Gloom. Once the Metin is destroyed, you will be teleported to the next phase.

### Phase 2
Defeat the [[mob:1093]] to get the ![[item:30304]]. Use the key by right-clicking it or dragging it to the [[mob:20366|Sa-Soe Tomb]] to summon the [[mob:1094]]. Defeat the boss to move on to the next phase.

### Phase 3
The Blue Room is where you face the toughest challenge of the tower: the [[mob:1095]], ruler of the Demon Tower.
