### Dungeon ticket
No ticket is needed to enter this dungeon.

### Recommended bonuses
Strong against undead.

--- {style=ornate}

### Phase 1
Destroy the Metin of Retribution. There are 7 in total, but only one will let you advance to the next stage. Find the right one!

### Phase 2
Choose one of the three Metins of Devils and destroy it completely. A [[mob:2591]] will then appear: defeat him, and repeat this for the other two Metins of Devils.

Finally, [[mob:2597]] appears after all three Metins are destroyed. Defeat him to face the mighty [[mob:2598]] in his lair.

### Phase 3
Defeat the final boss, [[mob:2598]].
