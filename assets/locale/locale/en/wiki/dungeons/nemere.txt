### Dungeon ticket
No ticket is needed to enter this dungeon.

### Recommended bonuses
None.

--- {style=ornate}

### Phase 1
Defeat all the monsters to obtain the ![[item:30332]]. Right-click it to make all the Arctic Cubes disappear.

### Phase 2
Defeat all the monsters and destroy the Metin of Cold to advance to the next phase.

### Phase 3
Defeat Commander Szel to advance to the next phase.

### Phase 4
Defeat monsters until you obtain the correct ![[item:30333]] to advance to the next phase.

### Phase 5
Defeat the final boss, [[mob:6191]], in his Ice Lair.
