### Dungeon ticket
No ticket is needed to enter this dungeon.

### Bonus needed
None.

--- {style=ornate}

### Phase 1
Defeat the Spider Baron to obtain the [[item:30327]]. Use this item to advance to the next phase.

### Phase 2
Destroy 5 Spider Eggs to summon the final boss, the [[mob:2092]]. Breaking more eggs will weaken her further.

### Phase 3
Defeat the [[mob:2092]].
