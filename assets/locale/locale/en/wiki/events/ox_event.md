## True or false
- The OX Event is a quiz: answer each question with **Yes** or **No**.
- While the event runs, [[mob:20011]] takes you to the event map.
- To answer **Yes**, move to the area marked O. To answer **No**, move to the area marked X.
- Players who answer wrong are moved to the spectator area.

--- {style=ornate}

## Rewards
The last player standing, who answered every question right, wins the event's reward. The reward depends on the occasion.

## Rules
- All in-game rules apply.

- No insults or toxic behavior, and no spam in the chat.
- Bots, hacks and cheats (a speed hack, for example) are strictly forbidden.
