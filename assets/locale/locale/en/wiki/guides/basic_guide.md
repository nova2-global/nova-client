## Hotkeys
These keys open the windows you will use most.

:::columns
- **U** opens the extra inventory.
- **F5** opens the switch bot.
- **F6** opens the [[page:systems/dungeon_manager|Dungeon Manager]].
+++
- **F10** opens the fast equipment changer.
- **P** opens the advanced pet window.
:::

## Finding your way
You can also find useful systems in your inventory and next to your minimap.

To read about any item in this wiki, hold **Shift** and right-click it in your inventory.
