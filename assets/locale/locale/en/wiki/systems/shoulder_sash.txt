## General information
- The shoulder sash is visible equipment, like your armor and weapon.
- A sash can absorb the bonuses of an armor or a weapon.
- Wearing a wing costume hides the sash, but its bonuses stay active.

--- {style=ornate}

## Upgrading sashes
- You can **combine** shoulder sashes at Theowahdan.
- Sashes of the same grade combine into a sash of a higher grade, even when their styles differ.
- Upgrading a sash that holds an absorbed item also raises its bonuses.

:::note
Keep the sash you want to keep in the first sash slot.
:::
