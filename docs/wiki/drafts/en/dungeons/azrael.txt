### Dungeon Ticket:

No ticket is needed to enter this dungeon.

### Recommended Bonuses:

Strong Against Undead

--- {style=ornate}

### Phase 1:

Destroy the Metin of Retribution. There are 7 in total, but only
one will let you advance to the next stage. Find the right one!

### Phase 2:

Choose one of the three Metin of Devils and destroy it completely.
A Tartaros will then appear, defeat him, and repeat the process for the other two Metin of Devils.

Finally, Charon will appear after all three Metins are destroyed. Defeat him to face
the mighty Azrael in his lair.

### Phase 3:

Defeat the final Boss, Azrael.
