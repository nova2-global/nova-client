### Where to get the entrance ticket:

No entrance ticket needed.

### Bonus needed:

Strong Against Devil and Half Human

--- {style=ornate}

### Phase 1:

Defeat all the monsters on the dungeon floor.

### Phase 2:

Keep killing monsters until you obtain the Crystal Seal .
Right-click it to proceed.

### Phase 3:

Defeat all the monsters, then slay the Crystal Beast to advance to the next stage.

### Phase 4:

Destroy all the Metins of Blood to collect Stone Piece .
Right-click them to proceed to the next stage.

### Phase 5:

Defeat all the monsters to summon the Crystal Dino, then slay him.

### Phase 6:

Destroy all the Metins of Stinging to summon the Crystal Dragon.

### Phase 7:

Slay the Crystal Dragon.

--- {style=ornate}

### Lucky Boss:

Lucky Bosses are rare enemies that appear after defeating the regular boss.
They drop the same materials as the standard boss but also have a chance to
reward you with an exclusive costume!
