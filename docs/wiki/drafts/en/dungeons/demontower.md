### Dungeon Ticket:

No ticket is needed to enter this dungeon.

### Bonus needed:

Undead

--- {style=ornate}

### Phase 1:

Destroy all the Metins of Ember and Gloom.
Once the Metin is destroyed, you will be teleported to the next phase.

### Phase 2:

In this phase, defeat the Death Reaper to get the Zin-Bong-In Key.
Use the key by right-clicking it or dragging it to the Sa-Soe Tomb to summon
the Elite Vile Demon King. Defeat the boss to move on to the next phase.

[[item:30304]]

### Phase 3:

The Blue Room is where you’ll face the toughest challenge of the
tower. The Blue Death, ruler of the Demon Tower.
