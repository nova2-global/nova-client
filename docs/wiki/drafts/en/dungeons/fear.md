### Phase 1:

Eliminate all monsters and destroy the Metin of Death to summon the Dead Tyrant.
Defeat him to advance to the next phase.

### Phase 2:

Defeat all monsters, then destroy the five Metin of Death. This will summon the Skeleton Lord.
Kill him to obtain the . Right-click the key to move forward.

### Phase 3:

- Destroy the four Metin of Fear to obtain the . Right-click it to destroy the first Monument.

- Continue defeating monsters until another Dragon Soul Stone drops.
  Right-click it to destroy the second Monument.

- Slay both Dead Tyrants to acquire another Dragon Soul Stone,
  then use it to destroy the third Monument.

- Finally, destroy another Metin of Fear to obtain the last Dragon Soul Stone.
  Use it to gain the Bone Key (30741). Right-click the key to proceed.

### Phase 4:

Defeat both Dead Tyrants and both Skeleton Lords.

### Phase 5:

Face and defeat the Almighty Fear Dragon!
