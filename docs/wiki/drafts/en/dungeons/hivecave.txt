### Dungeon Ticket:

No ticket is needed to enter this dungeon.

### Recommended Bonuses:

Strong Against Devil

--- {style=ornate}

### Phase 1:

Head to the end of the dungeon and defeat the Magic Monster to proceed to the next stage.

### Phase 2:

Destroy the Metin Cave Stone.

### Phase 3:

Find and slay the Bewitched Spider King.

### Phase 4:

Destroy all the Spider Eggs to summon the Enchanted Shaman Girl.
Defeat her to move on to the next stage.

### Phase 5:

Eliminate all the monsters in the room and then Slay the Hive Queen in her lair.

--- {style=ornate}

### Lucky Boss:

Lucky Bosses are rare enemies that appear after defeating the regular boss.
They drop the same materials as the standard boss but also have a chance to
reward you with an exclusive costume!
