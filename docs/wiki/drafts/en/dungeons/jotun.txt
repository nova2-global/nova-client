### Dungeon Ticket:

No ticket is needed to enter this dungeon.

### Recommended Bonuses:

Strong Against Devils

--- {style=ornate}

### Phase 1:

Destroy the four Metin of the Forest.

### Phase 2:

Defeat Commander Bagjanamu to advance to the next stage.

### Phase 3:

Destroy the four Metin of Tree Beings.

### Phase 4:

Attack Jotun Thrym until his HP drops to 50%, then destroy the Metin of the Forest.

### Phase 5:

Defeat the final boss, Jotun Thrym

--- {style=ornate}

### Lucky Boss:

Lucky Bosses are rare enemies that appear after defeating the regular boss.
They drop the same materials as the standard boss but also have a chance to
reward you with an exclusive costume!
