Players required : 4 guild members

### Where to get the entrance ticket:

No entrance ticket needed.

### Bonus needed:

None

--- {style=ornate}

### Phase 1:

Damage the dragon statues till it reaches 75% of its total HP .An Orange Shield
will appear which will protect the stone from taking more damage till you do the
same to the 4 of them .

### Phase 2:

Destroy the dragon eggs to get the shield off the statues and proceed to damage
the dragon Statues till it reach 50% of its HP.

### Phase 3:

Four Dragon Watchers Idall will appear,which will have to be defeated.
afterwards the dragon statues can be damaged until it reaches 25% HP.
Destroy the dragon eggs in order to damage the statues again.

### Note: Loot the dropped seals dropped

by killed mobs.

### Phase 4:

Damage all 4 statues till it reach 0% HP and get the Green Shield effect On!
Then use the Seals dropped earlier to destroy the statues

### Note: Each player can destroy 1 dragon statue
