### Dungeon Ticket:

No ticket is needed to enter this dungeon.

### Recommended Bonuses:

Strong Against Devil

--- {style=ornate}

### Phase 1:

Destroy all the Porcini Mushrooms.

### Phase 2:

Defeat the Oyster Mushroom, then use the Green Mushroom to proceed to the next stage.

### Phase 3:

Slay the Mushroom Officer.

### Phase 4:

Find and destroy all the Red Porcini Mushrooms.

### Phase 5:

Defeat the Mushroom Boss!

--- {style=ornate}

### Lucky Boss:

Lucky Bosses are rare enemies that appear after defeating the regular boss.
They drop the same materials as the standard boss but also have a chance to
reward you with an exclusive costume!
