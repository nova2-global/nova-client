### Phase 1:

Defeat all the monsters to obtain the . Right-click it to make
all the Arctic Cubes disappear.

### Phase 2:

Defeat all the monsters and destroy the Metin of Cold to advance to the next phase.

### Phase 3:

Defeat the Commander Szel to advance to the next Phase.

### Phase 4:

Defeat all monsters until you obtain the correct to advance to the next phase.

### Phase 5:

Defeat the final boss, Nemere, in his Ice Lair.
