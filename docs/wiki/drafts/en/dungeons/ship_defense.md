### Recommended Bonuses:

Strong Against Devil, Elemental & **Water** Element / **Water** Resistance

--- {style=ornate}

- Mounts are **disabled** in this dungeon.
- Protect the Mast; if it is destroyed, the dungeon fails.
- To start the dungeon, click on the Rudder.

### Phase 1:

Defeat the 6 waves of monsters attacking the Mast.

### Phase 2:

Kill the 4 Hydra Cubs and all other monsters, then attack Hydra until it reaches 50% HP.

### Phase 3:

Destroy the 4 Hydra Eggs and defeat Hydra to proceed to the next stage.

### Phase 4:

Kill all 4 Hydra Cubs and monsters, then attack Hydra until 50% HP.
Defeat both Siren Queens, then finish off Hydra.

### Phase 5:

Kill all 4 Hydra Cubs and monsters, then attack Hydra until 75% HP.
Destroy the 4 Hydra Eggs to stop the waves and defeat all remaining monsters.
Lower Hydra’s HP to 50%.

### Phase 5:

Destroy all Hydra Eggs and defeat the Siren Queens.
Attack Hydra until 25% HP.

### Phase 5:

Defeat all remaining monsters, then kill Hydra and collect the Hydra’s Treasure.

### Hard Mode:

Currently, the Hard Mode of this dungeon is identical to Normal Mode.
However, running it gives you the chance to obtain an exclusive costume
unique from all the others!

### Lucky Boss:

They drop the same materials as the standard boss but also have a chance to
