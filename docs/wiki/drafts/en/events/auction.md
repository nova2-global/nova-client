## General Information:

The auction system was born for 2 reasons.

1st: reintroduce skins that has gone very rare or even dissapeared with permanently banned players.
2nd: taking out yang and coins off the market and balance the NC to yang prices.

To access the event, you can click on the button that appears in the top right corner
next to the mini map

![](d:/ymir work/ui/auction/open_01.png)

---

--- {style=ornate}

Once you click that button, the auction will open up if there's anything for sale, otherwise you will receive
a message saying that there's no current items for sale

## Currencies:

Our Auction system is based on two currencies:

- 1. Gold (Yang)
- 2. Nova Coins (NC).

## 1. Gold (Yang)

This balance is withdrawn directly from your inventory gold balance and refunded to auction personal balance.
To receive the yang back from the auction balance, you can click on Manage Balance and withdraw all the yang.

Note: that if you have more yang than the maximum permitted amount (2kkkk) you will only be credited what fits

### your invenotry balance.

## 2. Nova Coins (NC).

This balance is withdrawn directly from your account balance (itemshop balance).
Therefore if you have any vouchers, you will need to redeem them in order to join the bid on the item you desire.

Note: The type of currency is defined by the server admins based on the market state and cannot be changed.

## How does it work:

When an item is added by the admins, a starting price is defined based on the market price of that item.
To bid on the item, you press on it and then press the "Bid" Button in the bottom left.

You should be able to see the starting price, current price and your bid.
If the current price is equal to your bid, that means you're the highest bidder and for that, stamp will
appear as follow:

![](d:/ymir work/ui/auction/highest_bid_stamp.png)

Once you place your bid, you can only increase it only if you have enough of the bid currency type in your
balance. Otherwise, you will have to wait till someone outbid you.

## What happens if you were offline?

If you were offline and you were the highest bidder on an item, you will be able to collect it when you login
again. If you lose the auction, the yang / coins will be refunded automatically into your balance.
