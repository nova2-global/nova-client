## Boss / Metin Spawn Event — The Ultimate PvM Challenge!

- **The **Boss / Metin Spawn Event** is a PvM battle where the Nova2 team summons**
  a variety of powerful bosses and metin stones for players to defeat!

- This event takes place in a special castle unique to each kingdom.

- While the event is active, you can enter the castle by **clicking the event button**
  located in the bottom-left corner of your screen.

--- {style=ornate}

## Event Rewards:

- Rewards will vary depending on which bosses and metins you destroy!

- At the end of the event, a World Boss. The **Green Almighty Dragon**, will be spawned
  in each kingdom’s castle, offering special rewards for those brave enough to face it!

--- {style=ornate}

## Event Rules:

- All in-game rules apply to the Boss / Metin Spawn Event.

- No insults or disrespectful behavior.

- No spam in chat during the event.

- The use of bots, hacks, or cheats (e.g., damage hack, speedhack) is strictly prohibited.

- PvP interference with participants during the event is not allowed.

- You may only participate in the event within your own kingdom.
