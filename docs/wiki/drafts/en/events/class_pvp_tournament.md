## Class PvP Tournament — Battle Among Equals!

- **The Class PvP Tournament is a fierce combat event that currently takes**
  place every **Saturday evening at 21:00 (Server Time)**.

- You can access the event through the Battle Bailiff located in Map 1 of each kingdom.

- This is a PvP tournament designed **exclusively for level 135 - 150 characters**, where players of
  the **same class** face off in one-on-one single-elimination duels.

- **Only one class competes at a time, and the order of participation usually follows:**
  Warriors > Ninjas > Suras > Shamans

## Event Rules:

- No potions are allowed.

- No HP fishes are allowed.

- No external buffs — only use buffs from your own character.

- Any kind of temporary or permanent invisibility from items or fishes is not allowed.

- Stealth (Dagger Ninja) skill is allowed, but using it for more than 20 seconds will result in a loss.

- Starting the fight before the official announcement will result in disconnection and a loss.

- Leaving the starting position early will result in disconnection and a loss.

- The use of horses and mounts is not allowed.

- Guild war skills are not allowed.

- Forming or using groups is not allowed.

- The use of bots, hacks, macros, or lag switches is strictly prohibited.

- Running into the arena before your match is not permitted.

- Sending friend requests, guild invites, or other interruptions to disrupt other players during the
  event is strictly forbidden.

--- {style=ornate}

## Important Notes

- All cases and disputes will be reviewed and decided by the staff team depending on the situation.

- If the “Teleport back” button appears before your fight starts, **do not click it!**
  It will teleport you out of the arena and result in automatic disqualification.

- If you register for the tournament and then return to the city, you will **remain registered.**
  However, if you are not present in the PvP Arena when it’s your turn, you will be **disqualified.**
