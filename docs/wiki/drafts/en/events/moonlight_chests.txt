## Moonlight Treasure Event — The Night of Fortune!

- **The Moon shines at its brightest, casting a mysterious glow across the lands of Nova.**
  With its light comes The **Moonlight Treasure Event!**

- During this magical time, you have the chance to obtain Moonlight Treasure Boxes
  by **defeating monsters** throughout the world.

- Each fallen enemy may drop one of these dazzling treasures, and upon
  opening them, you could uncover priceless rewards hidden within!

--- {style=ornate}

## Event Rewards:

- The Moonlight Treasure Event has unique rewards hidden inside the treasure chests.
  Each Moonlight Treasure Chest may contain a wide range of goodies.

- Chests drop based on your character’s level, divided into three distinct tiers:
  Level 75-119(L), Level 120-130(M) and 131-150(H).
