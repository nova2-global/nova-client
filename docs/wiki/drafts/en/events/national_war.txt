## National War Event — Kingdoms at War!

- **The National War Event is a grand-scale kingdom battle!**

- You can join the war through the **Battle Bailiff** located in Map 1 of each kingdom.

- This event is a massive PvP showdown between the **Red and Blue Kingdoms**,
  where every max-level warrior must rise to defend their realm and conquer the battlefield!

- **Once the event begins, both kingdoms will have 10 minutes to gather their forces.**
  After this preparation phase, the ultimate war for dominance will commence!

--- {style=ornate}

## Event Rewards:

- The victorious kingdom will summon the mighty **Lycan World Boss**
  a fierce creature guarding countless riches and powerful loot.

- Every warrior of the winning kingdom will also receive a **VIP Costume**
  and a **unique victory tag** lasting for 14 days to showcase their triumph!

--- {style=ornate}

## Event Rules:

- All in-game rules apply during the National War event.

- No insults or offensive language.

- No chat spam or disruptive behavior.

- The use of bots, hacks, macros, or any cheating software is strictly prohibited.

- Players are not allowed to intentionally feed kills or use alternate characters to manipulate results.

- Camping near or outside enemy spawn areas is forbidden.

- Staying AFK or inactive within the warzone is not allowed.

- Any attempt to exploit event mechanics will result in disqualification and possible sanctions.

--- {style=ornate}

## Important Notes

- Leaving or disconnecting intentionally during the event may result in **forfeiture of rewards.**

- Ensure your inventory has space before entering, as rewards cannot be **reissued or recovered.**

- Participation in the event signifies agreement to all event rules and guidelines.
