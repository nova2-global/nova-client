## Northern Light Treasure Event — The Shining Sky of Fortune!

- **The northern sky glows in its most radiant form, illuminating the lands of Nova.**
  With its brilliance comes The **Northern Light Treasure Event!**

- During this wondrous time, you have the chance to obtain Northern Light Treasure Boxes
  by **defeating monsters** throughout the world.

- Each defeated enemy may drop one of these glowing treasures, and upon
  opening them, you may uncover rare gifts and precious rewards hidden within!

--- {style=ornate}

## Event Details:

- To obtain Northern Light Treasure Boxes, you must be at least level 105 or higher.
  Additionally, only monsters within **15 levels above or below** your own will drop these chests.

- The Northern Light Treasure Event offers unique rewards that shine as brightly
  as the northern sky itself. Each chest may contain valuable items, resources, and rare treasures.

- Click on the chest to **view the rewards inside!**
