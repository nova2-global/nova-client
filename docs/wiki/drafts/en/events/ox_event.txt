## OX Event — True or False Challenge!

- **The OX Event is a knowledge challenge where players must answer questions**
  related to Nova with asimple **Yes** or **No** .

- You can access the event map through Uriel when the event is active.

- To answer **“Yes”**, move to the area marked with O.

- To answer **“No”**, move to the area marked with X.

- Players who choose the wrong answer are teleported to the spectator area.
