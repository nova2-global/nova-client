## 1. General Information:

The summer event content this year is made to fit all levels, this is why we created a map for each level cap.

## Low level: Lv75 - Lv109

## Medium Level: Lv110 - 130

## High level: Lv131+

To access the event, you can reach out to the event NPC named \*Etna Peninsula Guardian\*

![](d:/ymir work/ui/wiki/etna.png)

---

--- {style=ornate}

[[item:48365]], [[item:48366]], [[item:59086]], [[item:59087]]

This NPC offers you the following options:

## 1-Mystery Bay

## 2-Etna Crafting

- 1. This option will check your level and teleport you into the event map with
  accordance to the level gaps mentioned above.

- 2. The Etna crafting allow you to craft your hard earned materials into a long
  lasting rewards.
  That means, you can get permanent weapons, pets, mounts, hairs and costumes
  by submitting your temporary chests that you have farmed in mystery bay.

In addition, you may find other permanent rewards such as, Wings and effects.

## 4. FAQ:

Where do I join the event from?

- To join the event, reach out to the NPC called " Etna Peninsula Guardian ".

How long is the event gonna last?

- The event will last until September the 5th 2021.

Where do I find the bosses?

- 2 type of bosses exist on Mystery Bay, both can be found in the bottom part of the map.
