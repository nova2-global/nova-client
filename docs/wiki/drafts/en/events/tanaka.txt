## Tanaka Event — Conquer the Castle!

- **The Tanaka Event is an exciting multi-round challenge that consists of **three rounds****
  and on special occasions, an extra bonus round may occur!

- This event takes place in a special castle unique to each kingdom.

- While the event is active, you can enter the castle by **clicking the event button**
  located in the bottom-left corner of your screen.

--- {style=ornate}

## Event Rewards:

- The player who defeats the Tanaka holding the loot will claim the reward!
  Each round, three Tanakas carry treasure worth **500 NC** each.

--- {style=ornate}

## Event Rules:

- All in-game rules apply to the Tanaka Event.

- No insults or disrespectful behavior.

- No spam in chat during the event.

- The use of bots, hacks, or any unfair advantage.

- PvP interference with participants during the Tanaka Event is not allowed.

- You may only participate in the Tanaka Event within your own kingdom.
