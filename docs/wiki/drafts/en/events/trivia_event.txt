## Trivia Event — Test Your Knowledge!

- **Think you know it all? Prove it in our **Trivia Event****
  where speed, wit, and knowledge matter most!

- The Event takes place on our Nova2 Discord!

- We’ll ask random questions and the first player to post the correct answer in the
  **#trivia-event** Discord channel wins!

--- {style=ornate}

## How to Claim your Rewards?

- Winners will receive a private message from a Staff Member and
  an announcement with their name on that channel.

- You’ll then pick a number between **1 – 501**
  Your reward depends on your choice — choose wisely!

--- {style=ornate}

## Event Rules:

- Multiple answers in one shout won’t be counted.

- Only the first correct answer wins.

- You cannot change your reward once you’ve chosen your number.

- Answers sent via PM will not be accepted.

- Edited messagesare invalid. Double-check before posting!
