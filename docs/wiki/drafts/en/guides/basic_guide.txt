## The Basic Guide

- The following hotkey list should help grant you an smoother gameplay!

- Open the Extra Inventory
- Open the Switch bot

- Open the Dungeon Manager
- Open the Quest Manager

- Open Mystery Chests
- Open the Emote Shop

- Open Fast Equipment Changer
- Open Global Challenges

- Open the Wiki
- Opens InGame Voice Chat

- Open the Advanced Pet UI
- NEW Jump!

**U**, **F5**, **F6**, **F7**, **F8**, **F9**, **F10**, **F11**, **H**, **K**, **P**, **X**

- You can also find useful systems in your Inventory or next to your minimap.
