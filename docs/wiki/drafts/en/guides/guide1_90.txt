## The First Steps!

- Starting on a new server can feel overwhelming, but we’re here to guide you every step of
  the way! **Your very first recommendation is to familiarize yourself with the NPCs in Map 1/2.**

- These NPCs will become your go-to friends for upgrading both PvM and PvP equipment,
  crafting temporary items and buffs, and creating upgrade materials using the items you farm.

- The next step is learning how to upgrade your equipment so you can continue progressing
  through your PvM adventure.

\**At the start, you will receive the following equipment:**

- Lv 75+9 Weapon w/ 60% Avg. Damage + 20% Undead & Devil.
- Lv 61+9 Armor **(recommended to add Absorb. HP)**
- Lv 54 Heaven's Tear Earring, Bracelet and Necklace+9
- Lv 41+9 Helmet **(recommended to add Poison Chance)**
- Lv51 Nimbus Boots+9
- Lv41 Black Round Shield+9

- However, your main goal is to obtain the **Volcanic Equipment**
  Set and upgrade it to +9.

- It is also **highly recommended** that you remain at the
  **maximum Level of 105** so you don’t miss out on level-capped
  dungeons and Farming Maps that are essential for efficient
  progression.

## Where to Farm?

Using your travel to Farm Maps and then head to **Akanta Field** or **Akanta Cave** at Lv90!
![[item:70058]]

This map is ideal for farming up to a **recommended level of 90** while you complete your
\**guide quests**. It’s also the perfect place to begin working on your **Biology Quests**, as
it provides the required drops to get you started.

## Reccommended!

At this stage, you have reached the **maximum level of 105.**
It is strongly recommended that you do not level up any
further unless you already have the required items shown on the
right-hand side.

You can obtain the by defeating the following bosses:

\**King Wubba, King Crab, King of Lemures and King of Gnolls**

### Important Note:

If you wish to upgrade the belt to the highest rank of +9,
you will also need to complete runs of **Nemeres** and **Ochao.**

### Vote4Buff! (Recommended)

Lastly, we recommend you to obtain a permanent buff that will boost your damage!
When it comes to PvM, Strong against Monsters is the best option!
[[page:guides/vote4buff|**Vote4Buff**>]]

### 6/7 Bonuses:

6/7 bonuses are an incredibly powerful way to achieve a massive damage increase. While these
bonuses can drop in certain dungeons, your primary source will be in **Akanta Cave.**

\**For PvM, the recommended bonuses for all classes are:**

- Strong Against Monsters and Attack Value!

### Optional Progression:

If you choose to remain at this stage longer, you can also begin working on your **Advanced Pet.**
This is entirely optional and up to your preference—you may either farm the materials yourself
or purchase them at a later time.

### Recommended Advanced Pet PvM Attributes:

Strong Against Monsters, Strong Against Bosses & Strong Against Elemental.
