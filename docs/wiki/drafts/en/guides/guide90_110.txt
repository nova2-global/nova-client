## General Information

\**Congratulations on reaching this stage you’re doing great!**

At this point, it is highly recommended that you stop at **Level 120** before progressing further,
so you don’t lose important content before fully completing your equipment.

\**Once you progress beyond Lv105, the following content will **no longer be accessible**:**

- **Farm Maps:**
  Akanta Field and Akanta Cave (After Level 116+)

- **Dungeons:**
  Demon Tower, Spider Baroness, Devil’s Catacomb, Aqua Dragon, Solitary Temple

\**However, you gain access to the following **new content**:**

- **Farm Maps:**
  Lost Forest (Level 120–130), Land of Demons (Level 120–135)

- **Dungeons:**
  Hive Cave, Crystal Dungeon, Mushroom Dungeon, Duratus, Demon Pit, Fear Dragon

--- {style=ornate}

## The Goal:

\**At this stage, you should focus on obtaining the following:**

- Full Royal Equipment Set+9
- 25% Sash(Custom) with Royal Bow+9 (60 Average)
- Leather Belt +9 (two of them)
- Progress on your Advanced Pet
- At least Lv105 Biology Quest

### Important Notes:

- If any of your required items have **expired**, please refer back
  to the previous guide for their crafting locations.

- You will **need 2x** for the next PvM Belt Stage! (Demonic)

- ****All PvM equipment cannot be slotted with PvM Ores.****
  The only exception is PvM Belts, which can be slotted using Red Flame Stones.

## Dungeon Difficulty & Gear Focus (With Royal Set +9)

### PvP Equipment Dungeons:

- Hive Cave – **Easy**
- Crystal Dungeon – **Easy**
- Mushroom Dungeon – **Easy**

\**These dungeons are primarily focused on upgrading your PvP equipment.**

### PvM Equipment Dungeons:

- Nemeres - **Easy**
- Razador - **Easy**
- Ochao - **Easy**
- Demon Pit – **Medium**
- Duratus – **Hard** (Doable but slow)
- Fear Dragon – **Hard** (Doable but very slow)

\**These dungeons are essential for progressing your PvM equipment.**

--- {style=ornate}

## Important Item Drops:

Although upgrading your Royal Equipment only requires materials from
earlier Metin drops and a few runs of Nemeres, Razador, and Ochao, it is still
\**highly recommended** that you gradually farm **Lost Forest** and **Demon Pit.**

Doing so will help ensure you are fully prepared to tackle the **Demonic Equipment**
as soon as you reach the appropriate area.
