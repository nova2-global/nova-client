## General Information

In your first login you will receive a Level 75 Guide Quest,
these quests will teach you the basics of Nova2 all the way until Level 135.

--- {style=ornate}

## Lv. 75 Quest

- Task: Reach Lv.85.

It is recommended to level up in Akanta Field to get you a good amount of materials
for your first PvM Gear upgrade which you'll need at level 90.

--- {style=ornate}

### Reward: 50.000.000 Gold

--- {style=ornate}

## Lv. 85 Quest

- Task: Reach Level 90 and destroy 50 Metinstones of Metin of Ember or Metin of Gloom.

Both metin stones can be found in Farm Maps > Akanta Field.

### Reward: 1x Sovereign Sash(Fine), 1x, Thief's Glove,

### 5x Enhance Change Scroll+, 100x Clams & 75.000.000 Gold

## Lv. 90 Quest

- Task: Complete 15x Demon Tower and 15x Spider Baroness.

You can quickly access these dungeons by clicking on your keyboard.

Reward: 1x Lord Sash (noble), 1x Young Moa & 100.000.000 Gold

![[item:85003]], ![[item:52108]], ![[item:80007]], **F6**

## Lv. 110 Quest

- Task: Complete 20x Beran Seataou, 10x Devil's Catacomb, Razador, Nemere and Ochao and reach Lv. 115

You can quickly access these dungeons by clicking on your keyboard.
It is recommended you level to 115 by visiting the Lv.90 Maps.

- (Cape Dragon Fire, Nephrite Bay, Thunder Mountains)

Reward: 1x Lord Sash (noble), 5x Azrael's Horn, 5x Flame Stone,

### 5x Ice Flame Stone, 5x Jotun's Horn, 100.000.000 Gold

## Lv. 115 Quest

- Task: Destroy 250x Metin of Demise, 3x Metin of Treasures, Reach Level 130

You can find both Metin Stones in Lost Forest. Keep in Mind that Treasure Stones spawn in specific locations.
It is recommended you level to 130 in Guatama Cliff.

### Reward: 10x Multi-Crystal Fragment, 125.000.000 Gold

![[item:34017]], ![[item:1]], ![[item:80007]]

--- {style=ornate}

## Lv. 130 Quest

- Task: Complete 5x, Duratus Dungeon, 3x Fear Dungeon & Destroy 5x Metin of Treasures.

You can quickly access these dungeons by clicking on your keyboard.
You can find Treasure Stones in Lost Forest.
Keep in Mind that they spawn in specific locations.

### Reward: 15x Duratus Gem, 15x Fear Gem, 15x Treasure Gem,
