## General Information

Raid Set (Open World PvP)

This guide is designed to provide direction for your class
and showcase the best possible gear for it.

## Character Stats & Equipment

## Character Stats:

Max the follow stats in this order:

- Intelligence (INT)
- Dexterity (DEX)
- Vitality (VIT)
- Strength (STR)

## Fan (Situational):

Intelligence / Dexterity / Skill Speed (Casting Speed) / Vs Demi Humans / Crit.
(Optional: Poison is also viable instead of Dexterity)

## Reborn Bell:

Strength / Intelligence / Skill Speed (Casting Speed) / Vs Demi Humans / Crit.
(Optional: Poison is also viable instead of Strength)

## Reborn Armor, Necklace & Boots:

Max HP / Sword Resistance / 2 Hand Resistance / Arrow Resistance / Dagger Resistance

## Reborn Earrings:

Vs Demi Humans / Sword Resistance / 2 Hand Resistance / Arrow Resistance / Dagger Resistance

## Reborn Bracelet:

Hit Points (HP) / Vs Demi Humans / Magic Resistance / Absorb HP / Penetration Chance

## Reborn Shield:

Intelligence / Dexterity / Vs Humans / Chance to Block Attack / Stun Immunity

## Reborn Helmet:

Hp Regeneration / Magic Resistance / Vs Demi Humans / Poison Chance / Chance to Avoid Arrows

## 6&7 Bonuses & Extras

All items should have Intelligence and HP Including your costumes equipped.

## Other Equipment/Items

## Sash 1 (Defensive):

Any Reborn Armour with the Following Bonuses:
HP / Sword Resistance / Two Hand Resistance / Arrow Resistance / Dagger Resistance

## Sash 1 (Offensive):

Reborn Fan with the following Bonuses:
Intelligence / Dexterity / Skill Speed / Vs Demi Human / Crit Chance

## Ring of Defence+9 or Ring of Attack+9:

Depending on your playstyle, switching between these rings during PvP can be effective
Generally, stick to the defense ring to avoid becoming too squishy. However, in certain situations, like fighting
in the middle of a large group, using the attack ring for higher damage output can be more advantageous.

## Best Spirit Stone Combinations:

Weapon 1: Stone Against all Classes (Ninja, Warrior, Mage and Sura)
Weapon 2 & 3 Stone of Cooldown / Stone against classes of your choice.
Example (Stone of Cooldown, Stone against Warrior, Stone Against Ninjas, Stone against Shamans)
