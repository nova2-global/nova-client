## General Information

The Vote4Buff system gives you a daily buff to support your PvM or PvP needs.
It also helps Nova2 by boosting its rank on the Metin2PServer listings.

- You are allowed to vote every 24h in each character.

## How to Vote

![](d:/ymir work/ui/vote4buff.png)

When you enter the game, you'll see a Vote4Buff icon.
Click it to start the voting process. Everything happens right inside the game.

You'll need to complete a few captchas, and once you're done,
you can choose from 4 different bonus options.

- 15% Strong Against Monsters
- +60 Attack Power
- Max HP +2500
- 20% Extra Experience

It is recommended you select Strong Agaisnt Monsters for PvM.
