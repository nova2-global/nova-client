## General Information:

- Access the Battle Pass by clicking located near your minimap in the top-right corner.

![](d:/ymir work/ui/game/battle_pass/button_small_01.png)

- Free Battlepass can be started at Lv. 100

- Premium Battlepass can be started at Lv. 75

- If you start the Free Battle Pass and complete few tasks you can still start the
  Premium Battle Pass, but you will have to remake all the missions again.

- If you complete the Free Battle Pass you will **NOT** be able to start the Premium
  one until the next month, so chose wisely which one you would like to complete.

--- {style=ornate}

![](d:/ymir work/ui/wiki/images/battlepass.png)

## Tasks & Rewards

- Each month, you’ll need to complete a set number of tasks to unlock the final reward.

- The Free Battle Pass grants a temporary costume, while the Premium version rewards
  you with a permanent one.

- **Important:** The temporary costume cannot be transmuted onto another costume.

## Possible Tasks:

- Kill Monsters
- Kill Metin Stones
- Kill Other Players

- Damage to Monsters
- Damage to Players
- Craft Items

- Use Specific Items
- Sell Specific Items
- Destroy Specific Items

- Catch Fish
- Cook Fishes
- Spend Yang

- Farm Yang
- Complete Dungeons
- Mine Specific Item

- Complete Minigames
- Use Vote4Buff
- Refine Stones

- Combine Sashes
- Boss Dungeon Damage
- Add Friends

- Renew Offline Shop
- Online Timer
- Lose/Gain Karma
