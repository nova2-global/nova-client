## General Information:

- Biology quests present an opportunity to significantly enhance your damage output
  in the game. These questline consists of a total of 17 quests that can greatly
  impact your combat effectiveness.

- With the latest update, you can now purchase Biology items using farmable materials
  found in-game. Via the Menu.

- To streamline the questing process, you can conveniently use the key
  to swiftly deliver your biology items from anywhere within the game.
**F7**, **F7**
