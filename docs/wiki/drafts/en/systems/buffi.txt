## General Information:

- Permanent Buffis can be obtained from the ItemShop.

- Temporary Buffis can be obtained from the Shima NPC.

- Buffis come in two forms: Healer and Dragon & can be fused together at the PvM Crafter.

- Upgrading to Fused Buffi, will keep your +200INT!

--- {style=ornate}
