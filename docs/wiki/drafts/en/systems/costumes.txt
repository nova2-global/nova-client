## 1. General Information

## In Nova you will be able to find a massive variety of different costumes that not only can make you

## cool but also will allow you to add bonus to them!

## There's 2 types of costumes, the permanent and temporary.

--- {style=ornate}

## 2. Temporary & Permanent Costumes

## Temporary Costumes:

## If you're new and can't afford a permanent costume you can always craft temporary costumes in Lu Hao

## by exchanging for some cores which you farm throughout the server!

## Permanent Costumes:

## If you already have your equipment ready it's time to make some investment in your costumes and

## get something that will last forever!

--- {style=ornate}

## 3. How to obtain permanent Costumes?

## There's a few different ways to get permanent costumes:

## Shiva

## The Shiva NPC can be located in any major City.

## These skins never return after the month has come to an end.

## Battle Pass Premium

## You can preview the costumes by visiting Angelica NPC in any major city.

## If you purchase the Premium Battle Pass ticket and manage to complete ALL the quests,

## you will get a set of costumes exclusive of that month.

## Treasure Box

## While successfuly completing the battle pass quests you will receive Battle coins, with those battle coins

## and certain amount of yang you will be able to buy costumes at the Treasure Box.

## Events

## Events like Christmas, Easter, Halloween, etc will reward you with some festetive permanent costumes.

## You can also obtain exclusive skins by winning the Mixed PvP Event every Sunday.
