## General Information:

- Your balance is shown to the left of your Gold balance.

![](d:/ymir work/ui/gemshop/new/gemshop_gemicon.sub)

- Gaya can be obtained by refining Gemstones at Gaya Merchant.

- You can use Gaya to buy items from Gaya Market. Gaya can't be moved to other
  characters and can't be traded.

--- {style=ornate}

## How To Obtain Gaya:

- You can get 1 by giving 10x , 10.000.000 yang and any Stone +0 - +3
  to the Gaya Merchant located in (Pyungmoo, Yongan, Joan).

![](d:/ymir work/ui/gemshop/new/gemshop_gemicon.sub)

- There is a **50% chance** that this process will fail!

[[item:50926]]

--- {style=ornate}

## What can I buy with Gaya?

- With Gaya points, you can buy various items such as upgrade items, pets, mounts or boosters.

- The market is devided into 3 rows, those 3 rows indicate the value of the item.

- **1st Row:** Least valued items.
- **2nd Row:** Medium valued items.
- **3rd Row:** Most valued items.

- The 2nd and the 3rd row are locked by default and require to be unlocked.
