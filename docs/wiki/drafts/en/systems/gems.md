## 2. How can I get Gaya?

You can get 1 Gaya by giving 10x Gemstones, 10.000.000 yang and a stone +0 - +3 to the Gaya Merchant
(Pyungmoo, Yongan, Joan). There is a 50% chance that this process can fail!

---

## 2.1 How can I get Gemstones?

Gemstones are obtained from:

Water Treasure Chest, Earth Treasure Chest, Fire Treasure Chest, Wind Treasure Chest,
Rubynized Metin of Orcs, Garnetized Metin of Orcs, Emeralded Metin of Orcs, Sapphirized Metin of Orcs,
Green almighty dragon, Rubynized Orchestraitor, Garnetized Orchestraitor, Emeralded Orchestraitor,
Sapphirized Orchestraitor, Abomination Fusion and Violent Archne.

## 3. What can I buy with Gaya?

With Gaya points, you can buy various items such as upgrade items, pets, mounts or boosters.
The market is devided into 3 rows, those 3 rows indicate the value of the item.

- 1st row: Lest valued items.
- 2nd row: Medium valued items.
- 3rd row: Most valued items.

The 2nd and the 3rd row are completely locked by default and require Gaya Market expension to be
unlocked

![](icon/item/39064.tga)

The whole market will be refreshed for each individual every 4 hours.
If you wish to refresh the market before 4 hours, you need to use 1x Refresh Gaya Market.
