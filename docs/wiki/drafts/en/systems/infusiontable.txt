## General Information:

- You can Upgrade your Infusions at the Infusion table in Mythical Forest.

- The Elemental Gear is the only gear that can hold Infusions.

- The success rate to infuse elemental gear is ranges from 100% to 70%.

Infusion [I] & [II] **100% Success Rate** to add to equipment.

Infusion [III] **90% Success Rate** to add to equipment.

Infusion [IV] **80% Success Rate** to add to equipment.

Infusion [V] **70% Success Rate** to add to equipment.

- You can start by applying a lower-tier Infusion and upgrade it later to a higher tier,
  though this isn’t necessary. If you prefer, you can place a [V] Infusion directly from the start.

Note: The previous tier on the item will be **destroyed**

- The Infusion system grants unlimited power and boosts your overall damage,
  making it easier to clear elemental farm maps and dungeons.

- Once your item is fully infused, you’ll also unlock the ability to freely recolor it!

--- {style=ornate}

## How to Upgrade Infusions:

- Infusions offer a means to enhance your items in the game by upgrading them
  from level [I] to level [V]. To perform these upgrades, you can
  utilize the Infusion Table situated within the Mythical Forest.

- You will need spciecifc items obtained from the Mythical Forest Map, along with
  items obtained from Hydra, Ghidorah, White Tower, and Ancient Pyramid.
