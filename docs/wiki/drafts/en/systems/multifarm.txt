## General Information:

- The Multi-Farm Block is a system implemented in all maps and dungeons that enforces a
  limit of two characters per hardware. When accessing the server, the first two
  clients that log in are granted full farming capabilities, allowing them
  to engage in normal farming activities, deal damage to enemies, and drop items.

- Any additional characters that are logged in after the initial two clients will face
  certain restrictions. They will not be able to deal any damage to metins,
  bosses or monsters.

- The system exist for a reason, therefore any attempt to bypass the restriction applied
  to these maps will result with the termination of your accounts.
