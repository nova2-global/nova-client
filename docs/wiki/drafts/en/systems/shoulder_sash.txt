## General Information:

- The max. Absorption Rate is 25%.

- Upgrading the Sash with an absorbed item will also enhance its stats.

- The Shoulder Sash is a piece of visible equipment as the armour and weapon

- Wearing Wing Costumes will hide the Sash used but the bonuses remain active

- You can absorb armors or weapons to your Sash.

- Absorbed items can be removed with certificate of reversal or certificate of reversal+

![[item:85009]], ![[item:85010]]

- Using this item will remove the item,
  however it will **completely destroy** the item as well.

- Using this item will remove the item,
  however you will **keep the item** as well.

- Sashes of the same grade can be combined to craft a higher quality Sash.

- Same grade but different styles of Sashes can be combined together.

- A custom sash can be improved by combining it with another custom Sash!

\**Important:** Keep the Sash you want to keep in Sash 1 Slot.

--- {style=ornate}

## Bonus Absorption:

- (Basic) Sashes absorb 1% of the bonus from armour or weapon.

- (Fine) Sashes absorb 5% of the bonus from armour or weapon.

- (Noble) Sashes absorb 10% of the bonus from armour or weapon.

- (Custom) Sashes absorb 11 to 25% of the bonus from armour or weapon

- Absorbed items can be removed with certificate of reversal or certificate of reversal+

\**Note:** Certificate of Reversal will destroy your item, while Certificate of Reversal+ will not.
