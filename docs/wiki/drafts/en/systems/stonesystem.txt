## General Information:

- Stones can be upgraded through the Jae-Seon Kim NPC.

- Stones can be enchanted up to a maximum level of +6

- When socketing stones into your equipment, there is a chance the attempt may fail.

--- {style=ornate}

## Upgrading Stones

- From +0 to +5, stones can be upgraded using only Yang, though each attempt
  carries a chance of failure.

- Upgrading a +5 stone to +6 requires either or

[[item:34033]], [[item:34034]]

## Farming Stones

- Stones can be obtained by farming various Metin Stones.

- Left-click to see where items drop.

- Hold Shift to view the contents of a pouch.

[[item:51159]], [[item:51160]], [[item:51161]]

--- {style=ornate}

## Adding Slots

- The number of available stone slots depends on the item’s level.

- **Weapons:** Level 1-20 (1 Slot), Level 25-40 (2 Slots) and Level 40-140 (3 Slots)

- **Armors:** Level 1-18 (1 Slot), Level 26-42 (2 Slots) and Level 48-140 (3 Slots)

[[item:51159]], [[item:51160]], [[item:51161]]

- It is possible to add a 4th socket to your weapon or armor through
  Weapon Socket Enhancer and Armor Socket Enhancer.

[[item:50500]], [[item:50501]]
