## 1. General Information

## The Treasure Box is a unique feature that offers a collection of costumes and items for your

## characters. It serves as a repository of various costumes that are periodically added over

## time but never completely reset. This means that once an item is included in the Treasure

## Box, it will remain available for future purchases.

--- {style=ornate}

## 2. How to purchase costumes?

## To acquire costumes from the Treasure Box, you'll need two types of currency:

## yang and Battle Coins. Battle Coins, are obtained exclusively through the BattlePass quests.

[[item:34100]]

--- {style=ornate}

## 3. Important Information

## It's important to note that the items within the Treasure Box will not be removed once they

## are added. This ensures that players have a wide variety of choices and options for their

## characters' appearances, with new costumes being added periodically to keep the selection

## fresh and exciting.
